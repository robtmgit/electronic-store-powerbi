# electronic-store-powerbi
# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard – Electronic Store

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes sales performance for an electronic store using Power BI.
The goal is to identify key trends, top-performing products, and conversion behavior.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset includes:

* Product categories
* Sales transactions
* Product views (traffic)
* Pricing and cost data

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Data Modeling

---

## 📈 Key Metrics

* Total Revenue
* Total Profit
* Units Sold
* Conversion Rate

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Trend Analysis

* Revenue over time to identify patterns and seasonality.

### 2. Category Performance

* Revenue comparison across product categories.

### 3. Conversion Analysis (Scatter Plot)

* Views vs Sales per product.
* Helps identify:

  * High traffic but low conversion products
  * High-performing products

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

* The overall conversion rate is approximately **1%**, indicating room for optimization.
* A small number of categories generate most of the revenue.
* Some products receive high traffic but have low sales, suggesting potential issues in pricing or presentation.
* High-performing products convert efficiently even with lower traffic.
